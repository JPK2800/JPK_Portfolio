Research

In earlier iterations of the Ren’Py visual novel Library on the Lake, an archery minigame I programmed relied on players reading on-screen instructions to play as anticipated (Black Banshee Studios, 2026). Our studio’s realization was that players were not reading the instructions, so we used “gamification” by introducing a tutorial rather than relying on text instructions (Black Banshee Studios, 2026). Gamification to motivate learners, even within a video game, aligns with how Han and Chen describe gamification leveraging the inherent joy of play to create a learning environment that is both challenging and enjoyable (2024).

Research

During the early stages of our Unreal Engine project, Trick nor Treat, we faced a unique challenge of balancing the scares of a horror game with the education of a serious game (CelleC Games, n.d.). The decision was made to use quizzes as a test of player knowledge in Trick or Treat, similar to how the GMCLMS uses gamified quiz module (Ahmed et al., 2025). These quizzes are part of a system that improved motivation issues for learners in the object-oriented programming discipline (Ahmed et al., 2025).

Research

My personal project involves creating a prototype learning system in Godot 4.7. This system involves loading csv file modules, loading the learning material from these files, and implementing them modularly into a casual video game. I am applying findings from Li, Ma, and Shi (2023) to the project, including but not limited to discipline selection, target demographic, differences in learners, and techniques to improve intrinsic motivation. I will also be considering elements described by Han and Chen (2024) including offline leaderboards and missions as motivators.
